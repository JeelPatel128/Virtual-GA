Privacy

This chat helps you reach the front desk team. Here's what we keep and for how long.

What we store

  • Your chat messages and, if you ask a GA for help, your name
  • A phone number, only if you choose to leave one for a callback
  • An email address, only if you ask for something that needs follow-up (like an approval request)

What we never ask for

No passwords, student ID numbers, Social Security numbers, or financial or payment details. If a request needs something like that, a GA handles it in person or through an official Stevens form, not through this chat.

How long we keep it

  • A phone number you leave is deleted after 30 days
  • Your name is removed after 180 days, and the request is relabeled by number instead
  • What you asked about is kept for 180 days, then only a general topic and month are kept, never the question itself
  • An approval request's details (like an employment verification form) are kept for 1 year for our records, then your name is removed the same way

Questions

Ask the front desk team, or a GA, if you want something removed sooner or have a question about any of this.
